# AutoLazy

Less clicking. Less chat spam. A cleaner minimap.

For **World of Warcraft 1.12.1** with [ClassicAPI](https://github.com/brues-code/ClassicAPI).

- **Auto-roll farm items.** Need, Greed, Pass or Manual for Corrupted Sand (The Black Morass), Bijous and Coins (Zul'Gurub), Scarabs (Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj) and Wartorn Scraps (Naxxramas), inside their instance only. Every other item is left to you.
- **Accept bind-on-pickup.** The "will bind to you" warning is accepted when you loot or roll, solo, in a party or in a raid.
- **Hide roll spam.** The roll lines leave chat; the winner, the items you receive and money stay.
- **Shift turn-ins.** Hold Shift when a quest giver opens. Every complete quest is turned in, the one that asks for the most items first, and turn-ins you carry the items for (scourgestones, Armor Scraps, Zanza's Potent Potables) are done too, again and again while you hold Shift. A reward is taken when there is one; when there are several, the one you chose by hand last time for the same rewards. Accepting a quest stays yours.
- **Minimap tray.** The other addons' minimap buttons move into one panel behind the AutoLazy button. Pirate Radio and Group Finder can be hidden.

## Install

[Download AutoLazy](https://github.com/Fostercare5988/AutoLazy/releases/latest/download/AutoLazy.zip) and extract it into `Interface/AddOns/`. The folder should be `Interface/AddOns/AutoLazy/`.

## Use

Type **`/al`**, or open **Game Menu > AddOn Options > AutoLazy**.

The AutoLazy button: **left-click** for the tray, **right-click** for the settings, **drag** to move it. A click outside the tray or Escape closes it.

Zanza's potions: choose Spirit (or Swiftness) once by hand; Shift then hands in the rest for that one. Choose the other by hand to switch.
